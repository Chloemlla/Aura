Aura v6.45.0

Recent highlights: a new Rotation health screen in Settings under Diagnostics tells you why automatic wallpaper change is or is not happening, because the five reasons it can stop all look identical from the home screen and each needs a different response, and a Run now button lets you watch a rotation happen instead of guessing; a crash on Android 8.0 when a live wallpaper published its colours is fixed, since the call involved only exists from Android 8.1 and Aura was making it on every publish path; and the media, image-loading and networking libraries all moved up after a toolchain change cleared the blocker they had been parked behind, which also brought back a code checker that had been unable to complete a run at all and hid 52 real defects, now fixed.
