Aura is a free, open-source Android app for wallpapers, video wallpapers, ringtones, notification sounds, and alarm tones.

Features:
- Reddit-first wallpaper and video discovery from public mobile and motion communities
- HD and 4K wallpaper browsing from Wallhaven, plus optional Pexels and Pixabay catalogs with your own key
- Daily and community wallpaper picks from Bing, Wikimedia Commons, NASA APOD, and Lemmy
- Twenty-five Aura Original ringtones, notifications, and alarms that work offline
- Local wallpaper, video, GIF, ringtone, notification, and alarm imports
- Video loop and crop editor with frame thumbnails and trim handles
- Parallax and depth wallpapers using on-device subject segmentation
- Weather effects for rain, snow, fog, and sun through optional Open-Meteo access
- Time-of-day wallpaper tinting with sunrise and sunset awareness
- Dark and light mode wallpaper switching with separate slots
- Smart Crop subject positioning before wallpaper apply
- Optional generated wallpapers with your own Stability API key
- Wallpaper editing for brightness, contrast, saturation, blur, and filters
- Sound editing with waveform trim, fades, normalization, and format conversion
- Separate home-screen and lock-screen wallpapers
- Per-contact ringtone assignment through Android's contact picker
- Widgets, scheduled rotation, screen-off rotation, and unlock rotation
- Optional community uploads, voting, creator profiles, moderation, and sharing
- Local DataStore preferences. No ads, no tracking, and no subscription

Provider availability:
- Wallpapers: Reddit first, followed by Wallhaven, Pexels, Pixabay, Bing Image of the Day, Wikimedia Commons, NASA APOD, Lemmy, Aura Community, Local device media, AI-generated, and Open-Meteo.
- Videos: Reddit first, followed by Pexels, Pixabay, and Local device media.
- Sounds: Aura Originals, Aura Community, and Local device media.
- Legacy attribution only: Lorem Picsum, Internet Archive, Freesound, Jamendo, Audius, ccMixter, Klipy, and SoundCloud. Aura keeps source details for older saved items and does not fetch new media from these providers.

Content licenses vary by source. Aura keeps source, creator, and license details when providers supply them. Optional provider keys stay on the device and aren't bundled into public releases.
